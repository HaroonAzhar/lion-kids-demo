Where Do Lions Live?

The African Savanna

Most lions live in Africa. They love places called savannas. A savanna is a big, open land with lots of grass and a few trees.

It is usually very hot there! The golden grass helps lions hide when they are hunting.

Forests in India

Did you know? A small group of lions also lives in India! They live in the Gir Forest. This is the only place outside of Africa where you can find wild lions.

Where Do They Sleep?

Lions are lazy cats! They sleep for 20 hours a day. They like to rest under the shade of trees to stay cool. Sometimes, they even climb trees to sleep!
