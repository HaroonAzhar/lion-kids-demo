Lion Behavior

Living in a Pride

Lions are very social animals. They live in family groups called a pride.

A pride usually has a few male lions, lots of female lionesses, and their cubs. They love to protect each other and even groom each other to say "I love you"!

The Mighty Roar

Lions roar to tell other lions where they are and to warn strangers to stay away.

A lion's roar is so loud it can be heard from 5 miles (8 kilometers) away! That's really loud!

Sleepy Cats

Even though they are strong, lions are very lazy! They sleep for 16 to 20 hours every day.

They usually hunt at night or early in the morning when it's cool.

How They Get Food

The female lions (lionesses) work together to hunt. They are faster than the males.

By working as a team, they can catch big animals like zebras that would be too fast for just one lion.
