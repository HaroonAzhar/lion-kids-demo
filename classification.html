Scientific Name & Classification

What's in a Name?

The scientific name for a lion is Panthera leo.

Scientists use these fancy names so that people all over the world know exactly which animal they are talking about!

The Cat Family

Lions belong to a big family called Felidae. This is just a science word for "Cats"!

This means lions are related to:

  • Tigers (their stripy cousins!)
  • Leopards and Jaguars
  • And even your pet House Cat!

Lions are part of the "Big Cat" group because they can roar. Little cats, like the ones at home, can only purr.

Sub-Species

There are two main types of lions left in the world:

  • African Lion: Lives in Africa (most lions are this type).
  • Asiatic Lion: Lives in India (they are a bit smaller).
